About Prodort Spark

Built by a PM who's been where you are.

Prodort Spark is your trusted partner in navigating the world of Product Management. Founded by Nsisong Uko, an agile product manager who transitioned from a non-tech background, specifically classroom teaching, we understand the challenges and opportunities of this field.

Our mission is to provide tailored solutions and mentorship to help individuals and businesses build successful products with clarity and innovation. With years of experience in product strategy, execution, and coaching, we're here to guide you every step of the way.
